Signature Talks

Talks that help people understand how they’re designed to thrive

The Hand You Were Dealt

Most of us were taught one way to succeed, then spent years wondering why it felt so hard. This talk shows people how their design actually works, so they can make choices with more ease, more energy, and results that feel good to live inside. A strong opener for conferences and events.

How People Are Wired

For leaders and anyone who works with others. When you understand how each person is designed, working together gets easier. This talk teaches you how to effectively communicate with each type of person and how to be each other’s allies.

From the Poker Table to Human Design

At seventeen, I started playing in the underground poker games of Los Angeles, and by nineteen I was dealing them. For fifteen years I sat at high stakes tables, watching some of the most successful people in the world make decisions under pressure. I learned to read people well. Then Human Design showed me how to read myself. In this talk, I share the story of how I found my purpose, the moments that changed everything for me, and how I went all in on myself. I hand people the same insight that set my life on a whole new course, and they walk away knowing exactly how they’re built to win and ready to go all in on their own design.

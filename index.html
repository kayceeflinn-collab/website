My story

Hi, I’m Kaycee.

At seventeen, I was dealing at underground poker tables in Los Angeles. By nineteen, I was dealing the city’s most exclusive private games, including Molly Bloom’s legendary game, and went on to deal a private weekly game for some of the most influential people in the world.

From the center of the table, I watched how each player played the hand they were dealt. I noticed who stayed composed as fortunes swung, and who knew exactly when to hold, when to fold, and when to go all in. During the fifteen years I played and dealt poker, I realized it is one of the truest metaphors for life.

After years of all-nighters, carrying the weight of every player’s superstition, and chasing a relentless, intoxicating thrill, my spirit was quietly draining. I began to lose the drive to keep pushing myself in a world that gave me everything money could buy, and I couldn’t truly enjoy it. Then one night, as I was walking across Wilshire Boulevard, I was hit by a car going 40 mph. I flew 20 feet into oncoming traffic, was knocked unconscious, and broke bones. I came to in the hospital, and that moment changed everything.

It began a search for deeper meaning in my life, a path of awakening to a higher purpose. I worked with shamans, healers, and sacred plant medicines, studied Kabbalah, earned a Master’s in Spiritual Psychology, and completed yoga teacher training in Bali, all while still dealing million dollar hands into the early morning. Living between those two worlds taught me to stay clear under extreme pressure and to hold space for people at their highest highs and lowest lows.

Eventually, I took the leap. I sold everything, from my Hollywood Hills home to my Louboutins, and created a life outside the high stakes city, in nature, first in Washington, then on Maui.

That’s where Human Design found me. I was skeptical until I saw my own chart. Both my Sun and Pluto sit in Gate 28: the Game Player. My lifelong pull toward the edge had been written into my design from the very beginning.

Nearly five years and hundreds of charts later, I’ve never seen it fail to ring true. Poker taught me that every move ripples through the whole game. Human Design taught me to stop deciding from my mind and respond to life through my inner authority. The result, every time, is a satisfaction my mind could never have planned.

And no two designs are alike. Every hand is played differently.

Anyone can win. What matters is what lies beneath the win: the deep fulfillment that comes from playing your own hand correctly. Today, I work privately with clients who have already mastered the game and are ready to master how they play it.

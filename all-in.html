Questions
What is Human Design?

Human Design is a system that uses your birth date, time and place to map how you’re energetically designed: how you make your best decisions, where your energy flows, and the gifts you’re here to share.

What do I need to get started?

Your date, exact time and place of birth. If you don’t know your exact birth time, please give a rough estimate. There are ways to work with not knowing your time.

Do I need to know anything about Human Design first?

Not at all. Kaycee meets you exactly where you are.

Are sessions in person or online?

Sessions are held privately online, so you can join from wherever you feel most at ease. Ask about in-person options on your call.

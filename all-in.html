Questions

Before we begin

What is Human Design?

Human Design is a system that uses your birth date, time and place to map how you’re energetically designed — how you make your best decisions, where your energy flows, and the gifts you’re here to share. Think of it as seeing the hand you were dealt.

What do I need to get started?

Your date, exact time and place of birth. If you don’t know your exact birth time, don’t worry — we’ll talk through options on your discovery call.

Do I need to know anything about Human Design first?

Not at all. Whether you’re brand new or you’ve already pulled your chart, Kaycee meets you exactly where you are.

Are sessions in person or online?

Sessions are held privately online, so you can join from wherever you feel most at ease. Ask about in-person options on your call.

What is the investment?

The Deal · $2,500: three sessions of 60 to 90 minutes, used within 60 days. Includes a full breakdown of your chart, naming your natural abilities, revealing what has been hidden, and techniques to practice between sessions.

Retreats

The Private Game

An exclusive, fully customized luxury retreat for one person or an intimate group of up to six, designed around the people attending and what they want to accomplish.

Each retreat combines an in depth analysis of every participant’s Human Design and astrology charts with practical, high level work that applies those insights to how you make decisions, lead, and relate to the people around you. The setting, schedule, and pace are tailored entirely to you.

What’s included

  • Individual chart analysis. A detailed, private read of each participant’s Human Design and astrology charts, covering decision making, energy, strengths, and blind spots.
  • Group dynamics. For partners, families, and leadership teams, a clear breakdown of how your designs interact, where tension tends to arise, and how to work together more effectively.
  • Applied learning. Structured, experiential sessions that put your design to work in real situations, so insight becomes action.
  • Yoga and somatic practices. Private physical practices that support focus, recovery, and clearer decision making.
  • Five star care. Luxury accommodations, curated meals, and seamless logistics handled at the highest standard, so your time is spent entirely on the work.

Every retreat is individualized, limited in availability, and planned in direct conversation with Kaycee to ensure it meets your goals, schedule, and standards.
